# Ecommerce Content Strategy

## Objective

Turn customer demand, questions, objections, products, and search behavior into content that supports revenue.

## Inputs

Prefer:
- `.agents/ecommerce.md`;
- `customer-research`;
- product catalog;
- search data;
- social engagement;
- support questions;
- sales objections;
- competitor content.

## Content jobs

Every piece should primarily serve one job:

- awareness;
- education;
- problem recognition;
- solution comparison;
- product consideration;
- objection handling;
- purchase support;
- post-purchase education;
- repeat purchase;
- referral.

## Content categories

- searchable;
- shareable;
- commercial;
- comparison;
- educational;
- FAQ;
- proof/case study;
- product use;
- customer story;
- seasonal.

## Repurposing model

One useful insight can become:

```text
Blog article
→ Instagram Reel
→ Carousel
→ Story
→ Email
→ WhatsApp message
→ Ad angle
```

## Workflow

1. Identify business goal.
2. Load customer and ecommerce context.
3. Build 3-5 content pillars.
4. Map each topic to funnel stage.
5. Score by:
   - customer relevance;
   - purchase intent;
   - search/social demand;
   - product fit;
   - evidence available;
   - effort.
6. Build 30-day calendar.
7. Define CTA and target metric for each piece.
8. Reuse winning topics across formats.

## Output

| Topic | Funnel Stage | Format | Channel | CTA | Primary Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then:
- content pillars;
- 30-day plan;
- reusable themes;
- measurement plan.
