# Ecommerce Ads

## Objective

Acquire profitable customers, not just clicks or leads.

## Before starting

Read `.agents/ecommerce.md` when available.

Use:
- `customer-research`
- `offers`
- `channel-attribution`
- `channel-performance`
- `channel-budget-allocation`

## Campaign goals

Classify:
- awareness;
- traffic;
- lead generation;
- sales;
- retargeting;
- reactivation.

## Core economics

Monitor:
- CTR;
- CPC;
- CPL/CPQL when lead-based;
- purchase conversion;
- CAC;
- ROAS;
- gross profit per customer;
- LTV:CAC;
- CAC Payback;
- marginal CAC.

## Workflow

1. Define product and offer.
2. Define audience and demand stage.
3. Define conversion event.
4. Establish budget and economic guardrails.
5. Build campaign structure.
6. Create creative hypotheses.
7. Validate tracking.
8. Launch bounded tests.
9. Evaluate downstream economics.
10. Scale, maintain, optimize, or stop.

## Scale rule

Do not scale because CTR or platform ROAS looks good alone.

Require sufficient evidence from:
- attribution;
- CAC;
- gross profit;
- conversion quality;
- payback;
- budget capacity.
