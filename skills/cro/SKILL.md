# Ecommerce CRO

## Objective

Improve profitable conversion by fixing evidence-backed friction in the ecommerce funnel.

## Inputs

Use:
- `ecommerce-funnel`;
- customer research;
- traffic source;
- product/offer;
- device segmentation;
- analytics/session evidence when available.

## Analysis dimensions

1. value proposition clarity;
2. message match;
3. product information;
4. trust/proof;
5. offer clarity;
6. CTA hierarchy;
7. shipping/payment clarity;
8. form friction;
9. mobile usability;
10. checkout friction.

## Funnel routing

- low Product View → Add to Cart: inspect product page / offer;
- high cart but low checkout start: inspect cart friction;
- high checkout starts but low purchases: inspect checkout/payment/shipping;
- high conversion but weak margin: inspect offer/product economics.

## Workflow

1. identify the largest economic leak;
2. form evidence-backed hypotheses;
3. prioritize by impact and effort;
4. create tests;
5. define primary and guardrail metrics;
6. validate result.
