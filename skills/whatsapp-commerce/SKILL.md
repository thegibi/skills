# WhatsApp Commerce

## Objective

Use WhatsApp as a measurable relationship and conversion channel without falsely crediting it for all acquisition.

## Lifecycle uses

- lead response;
- qualification;
- product recommendation;
- objection handling;
- cart recovery;
- checkout recovery;
- post-purchase;
- customer support;
- replenishment;
- repeat purchase;
- win-back.

## Attribution rule

Preserve:
- first-touch source;
- lead source;
- WhatsApp interaction;
- conversion source.

WhatsApp may close a sale created by another channel.

## Workflow

1. identify lifecycle stage;
2. load product/customer context;
3. choose conversation objective;
4. use concise, helpful interaction;
5. track outcome;
6. attribute correctly;
7. feed objections/questions into customer research.
