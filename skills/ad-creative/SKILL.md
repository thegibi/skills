# Ecommerce Ad Creative

## Inputs

Use:
- product;
- customer;
- pain/desire;
- objections;
- offer;
- platform;
- winning/losing creative data.

## Creative dimensions

- hook;
- angle;
- problem;
- outcome;
- proof;
- format;
- CTA;
- creator/UGC perspective.

## Workflow

1. identify customer awareness level;
2. extract research-backed angles;
3. generate distinct hypotheses, not cosmetic variants;
4. match creative to platform;
5. define tracking labels;
6. evaluate using CTR, conversion, CAC, and gross profit;
7. keep winning concepts and iterate.

## Guardrail

High CTR is not sufficient evidence of a winning creative.
