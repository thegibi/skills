# Ecommerce Social

## Objective

Use social content to create demand, educate customers, build trust, and drive measurable actions.

## Content objectives

Each piece should have one primary role:

- reach;
- engagement;
- traffic;
- lead;
- product consideration;
- purchase;
- remarketing audience;
- retention.

## Inputs

Prefer:
- ecommerce context;
- customer research;
- content strategy;
- product performance;
- promotion calendar.

## Formats

- short video;
- Reel;
- Story;
- carousel;
- customer story;
- FAQ;
- product demo;
- objection handling;
- comparison;
- educational post;
- UGC concept.

## Workflow

1. Choose objective.
2. Choose customer problem/desire.
3. Choose content angle.
4. Select format/channel.
5. Define CTA.
6. Define measurement.
7. Repurpose winning concepts.
8. Feed strong organic angles into `ad-creative`.
