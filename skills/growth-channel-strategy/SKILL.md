# Growth Channel Strategy

## Objective

Turn channel data into a practical acquisition and conversion strategy.

## Core idea

Treat channels by the role they play:

- demand creation;
- demand capture;
- lead generation;
- qualification;
- conversion/closing;
- retention;
- reactivation.

A channel can play more than one role.

## Example channel roles

### Instagram
Often:
- demand creation;
- social proof;
- organic lead generation;
- assisted conversion.

### Facebook / Meta Ads
Often:
- paid demand generation;
- retargeting;
- lead acquisition.

### Google
Can be:
- demand capture via Search;
- organic discovery;
- branded conversion assist.

### WhatsApp
Often:
- qualification;
- sales conversation;
- closing;
- reactivation.

### Email
Often:
- nurture;
- conversion;
- retention;
- reactivation.

Do not hard-code these roles; validate with actual data.

## Workflow

1. Read `.agents/business.md`.
2. Map current channels and their roles.
3. Use `channel-attribution` to understand contribution.
4. Use `channel-performance` to measure economics.
5. Use `lead-source-quality` to measure downstream lead quality.
6. Use `channel-budget-allocation` for budget decisions.
7. Build a 30/60/90-day testing roadmap.
