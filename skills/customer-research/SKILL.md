# Customer Research

## Objective

Ground product, content, offer, retention, and marketing decisions in customer evidence instead of assumptions.

## Research modes

### Existing assets
Analyze:
- product reviews;
- support tickets;
- WhatsApp conversations;
- email replies;
- surveys;
- refund and return reasons;
- abandoned-cart feedback;
- sales conversations.

### Public signal
Research:
- Instagram comments;
- Facebook comments;
- Reddit;
- forums;
- competitor reviews;
- marketplaces;
- public communities.

### Primary research
Use interviews and surveys when existing evidence is insufficient.

## Extract

For each research source, capture:

- problem;
- desired outcome;
- objection;
- purchase trigger;
- reason not to buy;
- reason to return/refund;
- product confusion;
- comparison/alternative;
- exact customer language;
- content opportunity;
- offer opportunity.

## Synthesis

Group findings into:

```text
Pain points
Desires
Objections
Questions
Triggers
Barriers
Customer language
Content themes
Offer ideas
Product opportunities
Retention opportunities
```

## Guardrails

- Distinguish frequency from importance.
- Do not treat one loud customer as representative.
- Preserve verbatim language when useful.
- Segment by first-time vs repeat customer when possible.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
