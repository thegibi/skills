# Cross-Metric Diagnostics

Use these as diagnostic patterns, not automatic causal conclusions.

## CAC up + Churn up

The company is paying more to acquire customers who stay for less time.

Investigate:

- acquisition quality;
- targeting;
- campaign promise;
- onboarding;
- product fit;
- support;
- customer-offer fit.

## CAC up + MRR up

Ask:

- does MRR growth compensate for higher CAC?
- did payback worsen?
- did churn also rise?
- is growth sustainable?

## MRR up + Churn up

Acquisition may be masking deterioration in the installed base.

Separate:

- New MRR;
- Expansion MRR;
- Contraction MRR;
- Churned MRR.

## AOV up + Conversion down

Possible price sensitivity or product-mix change.

Validate before acting.

## AOV up + EBITDA down

Higher ticket is not translating into better operating economics.

Investigate:

- gross margin;
- discounting;
- product mix;
- COGS;
- freight;
- operating expenses.

## Revenue or MRR up + Runway down

Growth can still consume cash.

Investigate:

- receivables;
- payment terms;
- inventory;
- hiring;
- marketing;
- CAPEX;
- working capital.

## EBITDA up + Runway down

Investigate the bridge between accounting operating performance and cash:

- working capital;
- inventory;
- receivables;
- CAPEX;
- debt;
- taxes;
- timing.

## ROI up + CAC up

Possible explanations include:

- higher-value customers;
- higher AOV;
- better retention;
- better monetization;
- attribution effects;
- period mismatch.

Validate before scaling.

## ROI down + EBITDA down + Runway down

This indicates simultaneous deterioration in capital efficiency, operating performance, and liquidity.

Prioritize cash and cost diagnosis before accelerating growth.
