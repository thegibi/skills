# Ecommerce Offers

## Objective

Increase perceived and economic value while preserving trust and margin.

## Offer components

- core product;
- price;
- bundle;
- bonus/gift;
- shipping;
- guarantee;
- urgency;
- scarcity;
- payment structure.

## Offer alternatives

Compare:
- percentage discount;
- fixed discount;
- free shipping;
- bundle;
- buy-more-save-more;
- gift with purchase;
- loyalty benefit;
- first-purchase incentive.

## Workflow

1. Understand customer desired outcome.
2. Identify objections and friction.
3. Evaluate margin constraints.
4. Create 2-4 offer structures.
5. Estimate likely impact on AOV, conversion, and margin.
6. Test using `experimentation`.
7. Evaluate with `promotion-analysis` and `discount-analysis`.

## Guardrails

- no fake scarcity;
- no invented guarantees;
- do not improve conversion by destroying contribution margin without evidence;
- do not assume discount is the default solution.
