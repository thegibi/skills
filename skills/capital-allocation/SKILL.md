# Ecommerce Capital Allocation

## Objective

Decide where the next unit of capital creates the best risk-adjusted economic value for the ecommerce business.

## Capital buckets

- inventory;
- paid acquisition;
- retention;
- content/organic;
- experiments;
- technology/data;
- fulfillment/operations;
- working capital;
- cash reserve.

## Inputs

Use:
- cash available;
- gross/contribution margin;
- inventory position;
- inventory turnover if available;
- channel CAC;
- marginal CAC;
- payback;
- LTV;
- repeat purchase;
- channel capacity;
- fulfillment constraints;
- revenue/profit goals;
- downside risk.

## Workflow

1. protect minimum liquidity.
2. identify binding business constraint.
3. estimate return/payback for each capital bucket.
4. account for inventory and fulfillment capacity.
5. compare acquisition vs retention opportunities.
6. reserve an experiment budget.
7. create base, conservative, and growth cases.
8. define review cadence and reallocation triggers.

## Output

| Capital Bucket | Current | Proposed | Rationale | Expected Effect | Risk |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

Then:
- liquidity floor;
- highest-return use;
- biggest constraint;
- test budget;
- reallocation triggers.

## Guardrails

- do not allocate all available cash to growth;
- do not scale acquisition without stock/fulfillment capacity;
- do not treat projected LTV as cash already available;
- distinguish working capital from marketing budget.
