# Ecommerce Marketing Plan

## Objective

Produce a practical ecommerce growth roadmap tied to customer economics, channel evidence, and operating capacity.

## Planning model

```text
Awareness
→ Acquisition
→ Consideration
→ Purchase
→ Retention
→ Repeat Purchase
→ Referral
```

## Inputs

Use:
- `ecommerce-context`;
- `customer-research`;
- `channel-performance`;
- `ecommerce-funnel`;
- `product-performance`;
- `financial-health`;
- available team/budget.

## Plan sections

1. Business objectives
2. Customer/ICP
3. Offer and product focus
4. Channel strategy
5. Content strategy
6. Paid media
7. Conversion/CRO
8. Email/WhatsApp lifecycle
9. Retention/repeat purchase
10. Referral/affiliate
11. Promotions
12. Measurement/KPIs
13. Capital allocation
14. 30/60/90-day execution roadmap

## Output

### Current-state diagnosis
### Growth objective
### Channel plan
### Content plan
### Paid media plan
### Retention plan
### Offer/promotion plan
### KPI scorecard
### Budget
### 30-day execution
### 60-day scale tests
### 90-day strategic bets
### Risks and constraints

## Guardrails

Do not create a plan that assumes unlimited budget, inventory, labor, or fulfillment capacity.
