# Promotion Analysis

## Promotion types

- percentage discount;
- fixed discount;
- free shipping;
- bundle;
- buy-more-save-more;
- gift with purchase;
- seasonal campaign;
- flash sale;
- coupon;
- loyalty offer.

## Workflow

1. Define promotion and comparison baseline.
2. Compare traffic quality.
3. Compare purchase conversion.
4. Compare AOV and units/order.
5. Compare revenue and gross profit.
6. Compare new vs repeat customers.
7. Compare refunds and returns.
8. Check post-promotion repeat behavior.
9. Classify the promotion.

## Classification

- profitable growth;
- volume-only;
- acquisition-oriented;
- retention-oriented;
- margin-destructive;
- inconclusive.
