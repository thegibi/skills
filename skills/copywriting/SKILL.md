# Ecommerce Copywriting

## Objective

Turn customer evidence into clear, persuasive copy that supports purchase decisions.

## Inputs

Prefer:
- `customer-research`;
- `.agents/ecommerce.md`;
- product details;
- proof;
- objections;
- traffic source;
- offer.

## Principles

- clarity over cleverness;
- customer language over internal jargon;
- benefits connected to concrete outcomes;
- proof close to claims;
- one primary action per surface;
- message match with traffic source.

## Page workflow

1. Define audience.
2. Define awareness stage.
3. Define primary action.
4. Extract pains, desires, objections, and proof.
5. Write value proposition.
6. Build message hierarchy.
7. Write CTA.
8. Add objection handling.
9. Validate against offer and product truth.

## Ecommerce surfaces

- homepage;
- product page;
- category page;
- landing page;
- cart;
- checkout;
- ad;
- email;
- WhatsApp.

## Guardrails

Do not invent proof, scarcity, testimonials, guarantees, or product claims.
