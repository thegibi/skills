# Discount Analysis

## Compare

```text
Without Discount
vs
With Discount
```

Across:

- purchase conversion;
- orders;
- AOV;
- revenue;
- gross profit;
- gross margin;
- CAC;
- repeat purchase;
- refunds and returns.

## Decision question

Did the incremental conversion and order volume compensate for the margin given away?

## Guardrails

- Revenue lift is not proof of profit lift.
- Control for traffic mix and seasonality.
- Consider cannibalization.
- Do not assume future LTV offsets current margin loss without evidence.
